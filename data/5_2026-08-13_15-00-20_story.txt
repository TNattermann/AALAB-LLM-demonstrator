Once upon a time, in the quaint village of Nibbleshire, there lived a clever boy named Tobias. He was known far and wide for his wits and wisdom. One sunny afternoon, as he strolled through the village square, he overheard a ruckus at the bakery. Curiosity piqued, he rushed over. There, the baker, a rotund and jolly fellow, stood flustered, surrounded by townsfolk, clamoring for the famous sandwich which had just been baked.  
  
Tobias, always ready for an adventure, decided to help. "I shall create the grandest lunch feast with these glorious sandwiches!" he declared. The baker, delighted by the idea, handed him a basket full of the fresh, golden sandwiches. Tobias then envisioned a magnificent feast, attracting the townsfolk like bees to honey. But there was a catch: the only utensils he had were a spoon and a knife!  
  
Determined to make it work, Tobias stirred a huge pot of vegetable soup, but when he sat with his sandwich, he pondered, "How does one eat such a marvel without a hand?" Conjuring up the more ridiculous idea, he proceeded to scoop the innards of the sandwich with his spoon, while trying to balance the sandwich in the other hand.  
  
The spectators found his antics amusing, but the sandwiches squished and slipped, splattering the soup everywhere! Laughter erupted as Tobias realized his error. "Dear friends," he shouted amid the chaos, "never should one eat a sandwich with a spoon! For it should be held, not stirred!" The villagers howled in glee, and the lesson was learned.  
  
They all agreed: sandwiches were meant to be enjoyed with hands, celebrating simplicity over folly. From that day on, every time a new sandwich was made in Nibbleshire, they’d chuckle and remember Tobias's silly mishap.  
And the moral of this story: You should never eat a sandwich with a spoon.
