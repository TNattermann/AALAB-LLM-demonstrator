In a quaint village nestled by the hills, there lived a wise old farmer named Albin. He was known for his lush gardens and bountiful harvests. One day, as he tended to his crops, he noticed a rather peculiar sight. A brown wiggly worm wiggled its way across his prized lettuce patch. The children in the village watched with wide eyes as Albin gently picked up the worm to examine it.

"Why do you not remove it, Albin? It will ruin your harvest!" shouted a young boy, eager to protect the precious greens.

Albin smiled and said, "Ah, my dear boy, this little worm may seem like a menace, but it is a part of something larger. It enriches the soil, making it healthy for my plants. Take heed, for what seems troublesome at first can bring unexpected benefits."

The villagers were curious but skeptical. Days passed, and while they destroyed worms found in their fields, Albin let them be. To the surprise of many, Albin's garden blossomed beautifully, while those who discarded the worms found their crops withered and weak. Soon, word spread of Albin’s success, and villagers flocked to his home, asking for secrets to his thriving garden.

Patiently, Albin recounted, "Behind each worm is a treasure for the earth. Embrace what seems small or bothersome; it may just be of great value to your life."

From that day forth, the villagers learned to appreciate the small things, understanding that they often hold tremendous importance.
